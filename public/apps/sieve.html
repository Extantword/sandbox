Sieve

A sandbox for prime numbers. Write sets of numbers, turn plain numbers into sliders, and watch where the primes fall.

Sets

How expressions work
P, N
The primes, and the natural numbers 1, 2, 3, … up to the limit.
A ∩ B
Numbers in both sets. You can also type &.
A ∪ B
Numbers in either set. You can also type |.
A \ B
Numbers in A that aren't in B.
A + 2, 3A
Shift or stretch every number.
A / 2
Halve every number, keeping only exact results.
2^A, A^2
Powers of every number.
A + B, A * B
Every possible sum, or every possible product.
12 / N
Every exact quotient, so the divisors of 12.
{2..}, {1..50}
Ranges. Leave the end off to run up to the limit.
{3, 5, 7}
A list of numbers.
{n^2+1 : n in N}
Apply a formula to every number in a set.
{n : n^2+1 in P}
Keep the numbers that pass a test.
{p in P : p % 4 = 1}
Test only the numbers in a set.
and, or, not
Combine tests. Compare with = ≠ < > ≤ ≥, or write n not in A.
k = 3
A plain number becomes a slider. Use k in any line.
Colors
Click a set's dot to show or hide it. Right-click it, or press and hold, to change its color.
count(A)
How many numbers A has. Also min, max, gcd, floor, sqrt.

Every result keeps only whole numbers from 0 to the limit, so every result is a set you can use again in another line.
