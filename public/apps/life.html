Life

Conway’s Game of Life. A cell with two or three live neighbours stays alive; an empty cell with exactly three comes alive. That is all.

Patterns

Pick one, then click on the grid to place it. R turns it; Esc puts it away.

Rule

B is how many neighbours bring an empty cell to life, S how many keep a live one alive.
